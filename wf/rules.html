Section I: General Conduct
01. Respect

Be respectful to all players and staff at all times.

02. Zero Tolerance

No hate speech, discrimination, or harassment of any kind. This includes inappropriate builds or signs.

Section II: Land & Builds
03. No Griefing

No griefing other players' builds or land.

04. Land Claims

You can use /claim to claim your own private land, or by placing down a chest.

Section III: Combat
05. Consensual PvP

PvP is consensual only; both players must have PvP enabled via /pvptoggle.

06. No Combat Logging

Do not disconnect during an active fight. Hacked clients and exploits will result in a ban.

Section IV: Farming & Animals
07. FarmControl

This server is powered by FarmControl.

08. Animal Limits

Maximum 10 of the same animal within 10 blocks of each other.

09. Breeding Cap

Breeding automatically disables at 10 animals of the same type nearby.

Section V: Custom Crops
10. Custom Crops

This server uses Custom Crops. You can find seeds for these crops by destroying grass.

11. Recipes

You can find recipes for pots (required to plant custom seeds) and more in your recipe book.
